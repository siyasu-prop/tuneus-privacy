TuneUs Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

TuneUs is a music player that plays two songs at once, one in each ear. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. TuneUs does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal or usage data. The app has no internet permission. It contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking libraries.

Permissions the app uses

  • Music and audio (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): lists the songs stored on your phone so you can choose what to play. The files are read on your device only and are never uploaded. The app never accesses photos or videos.
  • Notifications: shows playback controls while music is playing.
  • Foreground service (media playback): keeps your music playing when the app is in the background.
  • Modify audio settings: sends each track to the chosen output.

Your app preferences, such as volume settings, are saved on your device. If you have turned on Android's device backup, Android may include these preferences in your personal Google backup, which is controlled by you and Google, not by us. We never have access to it.

Third-party services

TuneUs is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information when you download or update the app, under Google's Privacy Policy. TuneUs itself sends nothing to us or to anyone else.

Security

Because the app has no internet access and we hold no data about you, no personal data can be leaked from our side.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 give you rights to access, correct or delete your personal data. We do not hold any personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to provide or delete. You can contact us at any time with questions.

Children

TuneUs is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

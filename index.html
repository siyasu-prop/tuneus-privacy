TuneUs Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

TuneUs is a music player that plays two songs at once, one in each ear. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. TuneUs does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal or usage data. The app has no internet permission. It contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking libraries.

Permissions the app uses

  • Music and audio (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): lists the songs stored on your phone so you can choose what to play. The files are read on your device only and are never uploaded. The app never accesses photos or videos.
  • Notifications: shows playback controls while music is playing.
  • Foreground service (media playback): keeps your music playing when the app is in the background.
  • Modify audio settings: sends each track to the chosen output.

Your app preferences, such as volume settings, are saved only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Children

TuneUs is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
